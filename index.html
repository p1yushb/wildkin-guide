Welcome

Wildkin is live at wildkin-mu.vercel.app. Adopt a wild friend, grow its habitat, and turn real-life eco actions into progress.

Install it on your phone

Recommended: it then opens full screen like a normal app.

  • Android (Chrome): open the link, wait a few seconds and tap Install app, or use the ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone (Safari): open the link, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Laptop: works in any modern browser. Use W A S D or the arrow keys in Walk around.

Getting started

Setting up takes about two minutes and needs no email, real name or location.

  1. Welcome. Tap Let’s go. Returning players tap I already have an account.
  2. Age. Pick 12, 13–15 or 16+. This only keeps the experience age-appropriate.
  3. Quiz. Six quick questions find which topics interest you most, and Explore shows those first.
  4. Choose your wild friend. Sea Turtle (Ocean Reef), Red Panda (Forest Canopy) or Snow Leopard (Mountain Ledge), then one of three colours. The 3D preview shows it at home. This choice is permanent.
  5. Name it. Type a nickname or tap a suggestion.
  6. Create an account. A username and password, or Continue with Google. You can Skip for now and play as a guest, but friends and groups need an account.
  7. Save your recovery code. Username accounts get a code like coral-lantern-tide-4821. Screenshot it: it’s the only way back in if the password is forgotten.
  8. Meet your mascot. The habitat starts empty. Every real action you log helps it grow.
Another device? Open the link, tap I already have an account and sign in. The whole habitat comes back, and you stay signed in until you sign out.

Your home habitat

Home shows your mascot living in its 3D habitat. Drag to turn the scene; it also slowly rotates by itself.

Top bar: your nickname and level (tap it for Profile & settings), your Eco Points (EP), and your streak of days in a row with an action logged.

On its own the turtle swims loops and banks into turns; the red panda and snow leopard wander, sit, look around, blink and twitch their ears. Tap the heart to make it wave.

Happiness drops a little each day. Feed it to keep it happy; a hungry mascot moves slower and droops.

Each stage adds visible life: seagrass, coral, fish and glowing plankton on the reef; nests, bamboo, fireflies, a treehouse and waterfall in the forest; pines, a stream, an aurora, a den and mountain goats on the mountain. The mascot celebrates with a spin and sparkles.

Missions: real actions, real progress

Do an eco action for real, then tap I did it. It works on trust, with no photos or proof. There are 30 actions across Energy, Water, Waste, Transport, Food and Community, each with a line on why it matters.

Streaks: at 3 days in a row every action earns 20% more EP, at 7 days 50% more. Missing a day starts again.

XP raises your level, which unlocks the bigger habitat stages. EP is what you spend in the Shop.

Walk around and Build mode

Walk around (button near the top right of Home) puts you in control of your mascot on a wider island that fills in as your habitat grows.

  • Move: drag the joystick at bottom left, or use W A S D / arrow keys. The camera follows.
  • Jump (Spin for the turtle) and Wave are at bottom right.
  • Clean up litter: eight pieces appear each day with a glowing yellow ring. Walk over one to pick it up for 2 EP and a short fact about that kind of waste.

Build mode (the Build tile on Home) shows your plot from above:

  1. Tap a decoration on the plot, or one in Storage.
  2. Tap or drag on the ground. Green ring = the spot works; red = too close to something, too near the middle, or off the edge.
  3. Tap ⟳ to rotate, then Move here or Place. Store puts it away.

The plot holds 6 decorations at the first stage, rising to 18 at the last.

Explore: learning

30 short reads, five per topic, about two minutes each. For you puts your top quiz topics first; chips filter by topic.

Each read has a picture header, short paragraphs, a Did you know? fact, a linked source, and a Try this button that jumps to a related action. Tap Mark as read at the end. Some reads unlock as your habitat grows.

All 30 reads are drafts pending review. Checks on accuracy and tone for 12–16 year-olds are very welcome.

Friends and groups

The Friends tab needs an account. A small dot on the tab means something is waiting: a friend request, a trade or an unread message.

Friends

  • Add a friend by their exact username, or share your invite link or QR code. There’s no public search, so strangers can’t find you.
  • Visit a friend to see their live 3D habitat, level, streak and this week’s progress.
  • High five a friend once a day: you both get 5 EP.
  • Leaderboard: this week’s EP, actions or streak. Resets every Monday. Tap anyone on it to visit their habitat.
Which code goes where? To add a friend, type their username or open their invite link. The 6-letter group code only works in Groups → Join with code. The recovery code is only for resetting a forgotten password.

Groups (up to 30 people)

  • New group: pick a name and icon, then share the 6-letter code or invite link.
  • Chat: type, tap 💬 for quick phrases, or tap + to share your latest action, habitat, an article or the group challenge. Tap a message to react or report it.
  • Challenge: the owner picks a weekly team goal; everyone’s actions count; each member taps Claim for the reward when it’s reached.
  • Members: group leaderboard, invite code, and owner tools.

Trading and gifts

  • Swap food and decorations with a friend. Both sides must be within 25% of each other in value; the screen shows “Fair trade ✓”.
  • Gift items up to 200 EP with nothing asked back.
  • Up to 3 a day. Offered items are held until your friend answers; offers expire after 48 hours. Eco Points can’t be traded.

Safety and privacy

  • Stored: nickname, username, age band, quiz answers and game progress. No real name, location, contacts, camera or microphone. Google sign-in (optional) stores a Google email.
  • Chat filter: bad language is blocked before sending, including spaced (f u c k), symbol (f**k), number (sh1t), Hinglish and Hindi-script spellings. Phone numbers, emails, links, social handles and requests to meet or send photos are blocked too.
  • Limits: one message every 2 seconds, 60 an hour.
  • Block and report from a friend’s page (⋯). Reports are private and go to the Wildkin team. Group owners can remove members and delete messages.
  • Settings (tap your name on Home): nickname, password, recovery code, sign out, 3D quality, sounds, motion, analytics opt-out, and Delete my data.
